🌿 Health Tip of the Day

Start your morning with warm water – It helps digestion and detoxifies the body.

Do at least 20–30 minutes of exercise like walking, yoga, or stretching.

Eat a balanced diet with fruits, vegetables, protein, and fiber. Drink 2–3 liters of water throughout the day to stay hydrated. Avoid too much sugar and junk food to maintain energy and weight. Sleep for 7–8 hours every night for proper body recovery. Take short breaks from screens to protect your eyes and reduce stress. Practice deep breathing or meditation for 5–10 minutes daily. Eat slowly and chew food properly for better digestion. Stay positive and reduce stress – mental health is as important as physical health. 💧
