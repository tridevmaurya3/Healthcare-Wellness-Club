Shipping & Delivery Policy

Clear expectations for club pickup, home delivery, charges and delivery issues.

Availability confirmed first
01

Service area and methods

Available methods may include club pickup and home delivery to supported locations. The club confirms the method, address coverage and expected timing during request review.

02

Charges and timing

Any delivery charge is shown in the server-calculated request estimate. Delivery times are estimates and may vary because of availability, address verification, courier operations, weather or events outside reasonable control.

03

Customer responsibilities

Provide a complete address and reachable phone number. Inspect the package on receipt and report an incorrect, visibly damaged or missing item promptly with the request code and supporting details.

04

Failed delivery

Additional delivery attempts or address corrections may require revised timing or charges. Uncollected pickup requests may be cancelled after reasonable contact attempts, subject to payment and consumer-law requirements.
