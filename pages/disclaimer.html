Important limits on wellness information, product descriptions and individual experiences.

General guidance • No diagnosis

Not medical advice

Website content and club support are for general wellness education. They do not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a disease and do not replace advice from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

02

Product information

Use products only in accordance with the current package label and official instructions. Check ingredients, allergens, warnings, suitability and expiry before use. Ask a qualified professional when pregnant, nursing, managing a medical condition or using medication.

03

No guaranteed outcomes

Testimonials and member stories describe individual experiences. Results vary and no result, health benefit, income or business outcome is promised or guaranteed.

04

Information accuracy

Product availability, pack, price and offer details may change. The server recalculates configured offers and the club confirms current availability before final fulfilment.
