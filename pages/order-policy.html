Order, Cancellation & Refund Policy

How public product requests, prices, cancellations, returns and refunds are handled.

Updated 20 August 2026
01

Request before confirmation

Submitting the cart creates a product request, not automatic acceptance. Availability, current price, delivery, customer eligibility and payment are reviewed before confirmation.

02

Prices and combo offers

Displayed MRP and configured discounts are recalculated by the server. A combo applies only when every product selected for that combo is included in the required quantity. The confirmed quote or invoice is the final transaction record.

03

Cancellation

Ask the club to cancel as soon as possible. A request may be cancelled before dispatch or fulfilment unless it has already entered a stage that cannot reasonably be reversed.

04

Returns and refunds

Contact the club promptly with the request number, reason and supporting photos where relevant. Eligibility depends on applicable law, product condition, seal, expiry, hygiene and whether the item was incorrect, damaged or defective. Approved refunds are returned through an agreed available method after review.

05

Non-returnable situations

Opened, used, altered or improperly stored consumable products may not be returnable unless defective or otherwise protected by applicable consumer law. Nothing in this policy removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.
