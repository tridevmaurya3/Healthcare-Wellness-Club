Privacy & data care

Privacy Policy

How Healthcare Wellness Club collects, uses, protects and handles information submitted through this website.

Updated 20 August 2026

Your choices matter

Only provide information needed for your enquiry, membership or product request. Please do not submit medical records through general forms.

Privacy Contact
01

Information collected

We may collect your name, contact details, enquiry or order-request information, login and membership details, communication preferences, and technical security records such as login time and device/session data.

02

Why it is used

Information is used to answer enquiries, manage accounts and membership, process and track product requests, provide club support, secure the service, meet record-keeping duties and improve website operations.

03

Consent and choices

Where consent is the basis for processing, you may withdraw it by contacting the club. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully completed. Required transaction or security records may still be retained where applicable.

04

Sharing and service providers

Data is not sold. It may be shared with authorised club staff, coaches connected to your service, delivery or technology providers, or public authorities when required by law. Providers should receive only information needed for their role.

05

Security and retention

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used. Data is kept only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal records, dispute handling and security, then deleted or anonymised where reasonably possible.

06

Your requests

You may contact the club to ask about access, correction, updating or deletion of eligible personal information, to withdraw consent, or to raise a privacy grievance. Identity may be verified before a request is completed.

07

Cookies and local storage

The site may use essential cookies or browser storage for login sessions, security, cart contents and preferences. Third-party chat or linked services may apply their own privacy terms.
